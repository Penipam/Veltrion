Quantitative Implied Alpha & Pricing Lag Methodology

Institutional model framework developed by Veltrion Partners & powered by Gemini 3.1 Pro engine.

1. Mathematical Implied Base Odds & Alpha Model
Base Implied Odds = 1 / P(Polymarket)
Alpha Implied Odds = Base Odds × [1 + ((100 - PricedIn%) / 100) × (SignalStrength% / 100) × 0.42 + (β_stock / 20) × 0.22]

The Implied Value Alpha model quantifies the temporal lag between rapid prediction market re-pricing and trailing stock equity valuations. When prediction odds shift before stock prices adjust, the model projects asymmetric value alpha.

2. Dual Core Pillars & Correlation Signals
  • Polymarket Live Order Book: Direct stream of live event resolution probabilities ($P_{YES}$ / $P_{NO}$) updated in real time via the Polymarket Gamma API.
  • Market Priced-In Level (%): Evaluates 30-day trailing stock momentum against prediction odds velocity to calculate unpriced equity alpha.
  • Signal Strength Conviction (%): Multi-factor revenue and EPS exposure score derived from SEC EDGAR 10-K filings, corporate supply chains, and macroeconomic factor sensitivities.
3. Institutional Dual Gauges Interpretation
Low Priced-In (0–35%):

High unpriced opportunity. The equity market has not yet discounted the prediction outcome, creating maximum risk/reward asymmetry.

High Signal Conviction (80–100%):

High directional conviction. Corporate cash flows and guidance have direct material exposure to the event catalyst.

4. Event Beta & Stock Sensitivity Matrix

Each corporate equity is assigned a specialized sector event beta ($\beta_{\text{event}}$). For macro rate decisions, commercial tariffs, or defense spending approvals, the model computes price impact coefficients per basis point shift in prediction odds.
